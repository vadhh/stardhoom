# DESIGN.md: Afridho Tavadhu portfolio

Dev portfolio, one page: hero → Work → Experience → About → Contact → footer. `index.html` is generated by
`design-ref/build_html.py` (CSS, markup, JS) from `design-ref/content.py` (copy) and `content/experience.csv` +
`content/projects.csv`. Values below are read from that CSS.

**Origin:** the look came from a dark editorial "What I can offer" photography frame, recreated in
`design-ref/what-i-can-offer.figcraft`. None of its content is on the page.

## Mood

Quiet, monochrome, editorial. Near-black page, big tight-tracked display type, 1px hairlines that show the
column structure, a moving halftone field behind everything. Fira Code labels nod to the old terminal theme.
Contrast comes from size and weight, not colour.

## Grid & layout

**Desktop (≥ 1024px)**
- `.wrap`: max 1440px, centred, `--pad` sides. Four equal columns (`.grid`).
- Column rules: three 1px `--line` gradients on `.wrap::before` at 25/50/75%, full height of each block.
  Text spanning columns 3–4 (`.c34`) gets a `--bg` fill to hide the rule under it.
- Column helpers: `.c1` col 1; `.c2` col 2 + gutter; `.c34` cols 3–4 + gutter.
- Sections: 1px top hairline, 120px top/bottom padding.

| Block | Layout |
|---|---|
| Nav | logo col 1, four anchors spread over cols 2–3, "Start a project ↗" col 4 |
| Hero | `min(100svh, 1080px)`, content pinned to the bottom; name across all 4 cols on one line; lead cols 1–2; two CTAs cols 3 and 4; facts row on a hairline, aligned to the blocks above: "Based in" spans cols 1–2 under the lead, the other two sit under the CTAs |
| Work | label + "Selected work"; lead col 1; 6 cards in a 3-wide grid over cols 2–4; then "More projects" rows + CTA |
| Experience | label + heading cols 1–2, lead cols 3–4; job rows below |
| About | label + `h2.m`; `~/whoami` dl cols 1–2, prose cols 3–4 |
| Contact | label + `h2.xl`, email and offer cols 1–2; link list cols 3–4 |
| Footer | mono 13: © col 1, "Bored? Play snake ↗" col 3, "Built by hand, no frameworks" col 4 |

**Below 1024px**
- One column, `--pad` 20px, column rules and `<br class="wide">` hidden; horizontal hairlines only.
- Sections pad 48 / 64. Nav keeps logo + "Start a project", hides the anchor list.
- Hero drops the full-viewport height. The name wraps to two lines (one word per line, each about the
  container width at `25.5cqw`). CTAs sit side by side and wrap; facts become `120px | value` rows between hairlines.
- Cards stack (24px vertical padding); more-project rows stack meta / title / summary.
- About: prose moves above the dl; dl becomes `100px | value`. Contact email drops to 24px.

## Components

- **Hero:** `h1` is `white-space: nowrap` on desktop, sized in container units of `.hero` so it spans ~97% of the
  grid; column rules pass behind it. Space above the name stays empty so the background shows. Corner brackets:
  13px L-marks, 1.5px `--white`, top 96px (72 below 1024) at each outer margin.
- **Project card** (`article.card`, featured only, max 6): right-aligned mono index `01`–`06` → 16:10 cover
  (6px radius, 1px `--line` border, image at 88% brightness) → title → meta `STATUS · KIND · YEAR` → summary →
  `↳ highlight` → first 3 stack tags → links (live in `--text`, then `GitHub ↗`). No cover: hatched placeholder
  with the monogram. 1px bottom divider. Hover: title to `--white`, cover border to `--muted`, image to full brightness. No lift, no shadow.
- **More-projects row:** 4-col row between hairlines, 13px padding: `YEAR · KIND` mono (col 1), title (linked
  `↗` when there's a repo, col 2), summary (cols 3–4). Header shows "N more on GitHub"; ends with an "All repositories on GitHub" CTA.
- **Job row:** col 1 dates (`Mon YYYY to Mon YYYY`) + type; col 2 44px logo tile(s) → role → company → location;
  cols 3–4 summary, `↳ highlight`, stack tags, site link. Logo at 80% opacity, 100% on row hover.
  No logo: tile with the company's first two letters. Freelance rows are left out (they're under Work).
- **About dl:** two columns (term muted 14, value 15) between hairlines, under a `~/whoami` label.
- **Contact links:** rows between hairlines, name (Inter 600, 20) + handle (mono 14, muted → `--text` on hover).
  Email is 32px with a 2px `--white` underline.
- **CTA:** text + `↗` (or `↓` for in-page) with a 2px `--white` underline drawn as a background. Hover: underline
  retracts to the left, arrow nudges 2px up-right (`↓` drops 3px).

## Motion & background

GSAP 3.15.0 (core, ScrollTrigger, SplitText, ScrambleText) + Lenis 1.3.27, vendored in `js/`. Only transform,
opacity and clip-path animate; each effect plays once.

- **Load:** column rules draw down; the name rises letter by letter out of its line mask; lead lines, both CTAs
  (underline drawing in) and facts fade up; corner brackets land last.
- **Scroll:** labels and card indexes scramble in; cards fade up while covers wipe up and images settle from 1.15×;
  job rows slide up; numbers in `↳` highlights count up.
- **Smooth scroll:** Lenis on GSAP's ticker, anchor jumps glide. In-page links use a custom click handler (`lenis.scrollTo`, then focus the target with
  `preventScroll` and `pushState` the hash); Lenis's own `anchors` option is off.
- **Background warp:** fixed full-screen canvas behind the page, 12px halftone dot grid. Dot size follows a
  domain-warped field (grid bent twice by slow sines, read as marbling) that travels diagonally and is pushed by
  scroll. Dots swell within 160px of the pointer; a `--pop` glint crosses the brightest crests. Sweeps in from the
  left after the intro, 50% opacity in the hero, full one screen down.
  Drawn at ~30fps, skipped while the tab is hidden; colours come from `--text` / `--pop` read once at start.
- **Hiding:** `.js` on `<html>` hides hero parts until the intro starts and removes itself after 3s; if the
  animation clock stalls, everything reverts to visible.

## Accessibility

- `:focus-visible`: 2px `--white` outline, 3px offset. Sections are `aria-labelledby` their `h2`; decorative
  indexes, corners and the canvas are `aria-hidden`.
- "Skip to content" link to `main#main` is the first focusable element, hidden above the viewport until focused.
- `#top`, `main` and the four sections carry `tabindex="-1"` so anchor jumps move focus (no ring on them).
- Below 1024px, every link (nav, hero CTAs, email, `.link`, `.rows .title`, footer) gets padding + negative
  margin for a ≥40px hit area without moving the layout.
- `color-scheme: dark` on `:root`.
- Reduced motion keeps colour, border, opacity and filter fades (≤200ms) but drops transforms, GSAP motion,
  scroll smoothing and the moving background (drawn once, still).
- **No JS:** the full page renders statically; no background canvas.

## Building the site

`python3 design-ref/build_html.py` writes `index.html` (don't hand-edit it) and asserts card count, hidden
projects, and that every listed image, logo and `js/` file exists. Run with plain `python3`, not `-I`.

- `projects.csv`: `order,title,kind,status,year,summary,highlight,stack,repo,monogram,show,image,live`.
  `show` = `featured` → card (max 6; if none, the first six not `ARCHIVED`), `no` → hidden, blank → "More projects"
  (newest year first). `stack` is `;`-separated. `image` → 16:10 cover, 960×600 JPEG/PNG in `img/projects/`.
- `experience.csv`: `order,type,role,company,start,end,location,summary,highlight,stack,link,logo`.
  Dates `YYYY-MM` or `Present`. `logo` → 96×96 PNG(s) in `img/logos/`, `;`-separated for side-by-side tiles.
- Copy (hero lead, section leads, about, whoami, facts, offer, email, links) lives in `content.py`. A `\n` is a
  desktop-only line break.
- Upload to Neocities: `index.html`, `js/`, `img/projects/`, `img/logos/`, `snake.html` (footer link). Not
  `design-ref/`: it holds crops of a third-party screenshot. Nothing runs on the host.
