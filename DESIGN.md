# DESIGN.md: Afridho Tavadhu portfolio

Dev portfolio, one page: hero → Work → Experience → About → Contact → footer → closing flower. `index.html` is generated by
`design-ref/build_html.py` (CSS and markup, with `design-ref/anim.js` inlined as its script) from `design-ref/content.py` (copy) and `content/experience.csv` +
`content/projects.csv`. Values below are read from that CSS.

**Origin:** the look came from a dark editorial "What I can offer" photography frame, recreated in
`design-ref/what-i-can-offer.figcraft`. None of its content is on the page.

## Mood

Quiet, monochrome, editorial. Near-black page, big tight-tracked display type, 1px hairlines that show the
column structure, a batik stippled in dots breathing behind the hero. Fira Code labels nod to the old terminal theme.
Contrast comes from size and weight, not colour.

## Typography

**Inter** 400/600 for everything; **Fira Code** 400 for labels, indexes, dates, tags and meta. Both self-hosted
from `fonts/` (latin subsets, SIL OFL in `fonts/OFL.txt`): Inter is one variable woff2 for 400–600, both files
are preloaded, `font-display: swap`, and a size-matched local Arial ("Inter Fallback") stands in while Inter loads.
`↗` and `↳` aren't in either font, so they come from the system face. Tight negative tracking on all display sizes.
Sizes are set in rem (the table gives px at the default 16px root); fluid sizes clamp between rem bounds no more than
2.5x apart, with a small rem share in the slope, equal to the old px/vw curves at 390 and 1440 and within ~2px between.

## Grid & layout

**Desktop (≥ 1024px)**
- `.wrap`: max 1440px, centred, `--pad` sides. Four equal columns (`.grid`).
- Column rules: three 1px `--line` gradients on `.wrap::before` at 25/50/75%, full height of each block.
  Text spanning columns 3–4 (`.c34`) gets a `--bg` fill to hide the rule under it.
- Column helpers: `.c1` col 1; `.c2` col 2 + gutter; `.c34` cols 3–4 + gutter.
- Sections: 1px top hairline, 120px top/bottom padding.

| Block | Layout |
|---|---|
| Nav | logo col 1, four anchors spread over cols 2–3, "Start a project ↗" col 4 |
| Hero | `min(100svh, 1080px)`, content pinned to the bottom; name across all 4 cols on one line; lead cols 1–2; two CTAs cols 3 and 4; facts row on a hairline, aligned to the blocks above: "Based in" spans cols 1–2 under the lead, the other two sit under the CTAs |
| Work | label + "Selected work"; lead col 1; 6 cards in a 3-wide grid over cols 2–4; then "More projects" rows + CTA |
| Experience | label + heading cols 1–2, lead cols 3–4; job rows below |
| About | label + `h2.m`; `~/whoami` dl cols 1–2, prose cols 3–4 |
| Contact | label + `h2.xl`, email and offer cols 1–2; link list cols 3–4 |
| Footer | the `<footer>` landmark holds this row and the closing flower. Mono 13: © col 1, the batik's name and its photo's source (@katalogsuminarkediri on Instagram) col 2, "Built by hand, no frameworks" col 4 |
| Closing flower | `.flower-end`, full width, one screen (`100svh`) past the footer: the flower in glass tiles on a canvas over its poster, fading in from `--bg` over its top 15%. No text on it |

**Below 1024px**
- One column, `--pad` 20px, column rules and `<br class="wide">` hidden; horizontal hairlines only.
- Sections pad 48 / 64. Nav keeps logo + "Start a project", hides the anchor list.
- Hero drops the full-viewport height. The name wraps to two lines (one word per line, each about the
  container width at `25.5cqw`, at every width below 1024). CTAs sit side by side and wrap; facts become `120px | value` rows between hairlines.
- Cards stack (24px vertical padding); more-project rows stack meta / title / summary.
- About: prose moves above the dl; dl becomes `100px | value`. Contact email drops to 24px.

## Components

- **Hero:** `h1` is `white-space: nowrap` on desktop, sized in container units of `.hero` so it spans ~97% of the
  grid; column rules pass behind it. Space above the name stays empty so the background shows. Corner brackets:
  13px L-marks, 1.5px `--white`, top 96px (72 below 1024) at each outer margin.
- **Project card** (`article.card`, featured only, max 6): right-aligned mono index `01`–`06` → 16:10 cover
  (6px radius, 1px `--line` border, image at 88% brightness) → title → meta `STATUS · KIND · YEAR` → summary →
  `↳ highlight` → first 3 stack tags → links (live in `--text`, then `GitHub ↗`). No cover: hatched placeholder
  with the monogram. 1px bottom divider. Hover, or keyboard focus on a link in the card: title to `--white`, cover border to
  `--muted`, image to full brightness. No lift, no shadow.
- **More-projects row:** 4-col row between hairlines, 13px padding: `YEAR · KIND` mono (col 1), title (linked
  `↗` when there's a repo, col 2), summary (cols 3–4). Header shows "N more on GitHub"; ends with an "All repositories on GitHub" CTA.
- **Job row:** col 1 dates (`Mon YYYY to Mon YYYY`) + type; col 2 44px logo tile(s) → role → company → location;
  cols 3–4 summary, `↳ highlight`, stack tags, site link. Logo at 80% opacity, 100% on row hover or keyboard focus in the row.
  No logo: tile with the company's first two letters. Freelance rows are left out (they're under Work).
- **About dl:** two columns (term muted 14, value 15) between hairlines, under a `~/whoami` label.
- **Contact links:** rows between hairlines, name (Inter 600, 20) + handle (mono 14, muted → `--text` on hover).
  Email is 32px with a 2px `--white` underline, drawn as a pseudo-element so it can draw in on scroll and retract to the
  left on hover (real-hover devices only, since a tap opens Mail) or keyboard focus.
- **CTA:** text + `↗` (or `↓` for in-page) with a 2px `--white` underline, a border on `::before` scaled by `--u` like the
  email's (so it shows in forced colours and print). Hover or keyboard
  focus: underline retracts to the left, arrow nudges 2px up-right (`↓` drops 3px; motion allowed only). Press: CTAs turn
  `--muted`, text links dim to 60%, the pause buttons shrink to 90%. Every hover on the page has a
  `:focus-visible` twin; card and job rows use `:has(:focus-visible)`, so a mouse click doesn't leave them lit.

## Motion & background

GSAP 3.15.0 (core, ScrollTrigger, SplitText, ScrambleText) + Lenis 1.3.27, vendored in `js/`. Only transform,
opacity and clip-path animate. Each effect plays once, except the scroll-linked parallax and the hero's background.

- **Load:** column rules draw down; the name rises letter by letter out of its line mask; lead lines, both CTAs
  (underline drawing in) and facts fade up; corner brackets land last.
- **Layers:** the hero text that moves (the name's letters and its parallax, the lead, CTAs and facts) has
  `will-change: transform` (motion allowed only), so it is drawn once with its six-shadow halo and then moved by the
  compositor. Redrawing the blurred halo every frame held the GPU at ~85% and dropped about a third of the frames on load
  and on scrolling out of the hero (headed Brave on an M1; headless never showed it).
- **Scroll:** labels and card indexes scramble in; cards fade up while covers wipe up and images settle from 1.15×;
  job rows slide up. Cards and job rows fade with opacity, not visibility, so their links stay in the Tab order before
  they scroll in. Multi-digit numbers in `↳` highlights count up (a single digit would flash false values like
  "under 0 second"). The real text stays in the DOM until the count starts: it is zeroed only
  when the trigger fires, then ends on the exact original text.
- **Contact bookend:** the closing line rises letter by letter like the name (SplitText line masks,
  padded `.ln-mask` so the g's tail isn't clipped, which is why it starts at 125% rather than 110%: the i dots stay hidden; a space before its `<br>` keeps the aria-label "Let's build
  something."). Then the email underline draws in from the left (`--u` scales `.email::after`); its trigger is clamped so it
  still fires on screens too tall for the email to reach 85%.
- **Parallax:** on desktop (≥1024px) the name lifts out faster than the page, up to 25% of the hero's height as the hero scrolls away;
  it's scroll-linked, so scrolling back restores it. It is a CSS scroll-driven animation (`h1 { animation: lift }` on
  `scroll(root)`, `translate` over the first `min(100svh, 1080px)` of scroll, the hero's height whenever its content
  fits), which follows Lenis since Lenis scrolls the document. Where `animation-timeline` isn't supported, anim.js scrubs
  the same lift with GSAP ScrollTrigger: one trigger at every width with a distance of 0 below 1024px (a width query
  would reset the scroll position when the window widens past 1024px).
- **Smooth scroll:** Lenis on GSAP's ticker, anchor jumps glide. In-page links use a custom click handler (`lenis.scrollTo`, then focus the target with
  `preventScroll` and `pushState` the hash); Lenis's own `anchors` option is off.
- **Snap per section:** one swipe, one stop. On a swipe's first wheel event, if the next stop in its direction is at
  most a screen away, the page glides there at once (0.6s, ease-out quart). The rest of the swipe, momentum included,
  is swallowed until the wheel has rested 150ms, and the page never pulls back against the swipe. A stop further away
  (inside Work and Experience, taller than a screen) leaves the page scrolling freely, so a glide never skips what
  hasn't been on screen. Stops: the tops of the hero and the four sections; past the footer, the closing flower by its
  bottom edge twice, first at a zero-height `.third` marker a third of the way down it (its top third showing under
  the footer), then at its own foot (the whole flower). It is a hook on Lenis' wheel input (`virtualScroll`), not Lenis'
  snap module: in proximity mode the module waited half a second after the wheel rested, then pulled a short swipe
  back where it started; its lock mode aims past where a swipe ends, so a long swipe skips the closing screen's one-third rest. Wheel and trackpad only: touch, keys and
  anchor jumps scroll as they are; sideways swipes and the 1-3px of a resting finger pass through; reduced motion has
  no snap (no Lenis).
- **Closing flower:** a reel's halftone of a flower (64×36 white squares on a ~20px grid, a square's size its
  brightness; 79 frames at 15 fps), used with its authors' permission, traced cell by cell by
  `design-ref/trace_flower.py` into `img/footer-flower.png` (79 KB) and drawn live by a WebGL shader (`FLOWER_FS`,
  `endFlower()`) as frosted glass tiles after a glass-tile reference: rounded tiles 0.92 of the cell, a thin rim lit
  top left, a brighter inner edge and a sheen, the tone glowing through the gaps on `--bg`. Brightness and colour
  follow the reel's tone (its square's area): deep blue where it is dim, cyan, ice white where it is brightest, the
  reference's blue/green backdrop mixed in a third. Colour by screen position made it a silhouette; this reads as petals
  round a glowing centre. The grid covers the screen (a tall one shows its middle 30 columns, the empty tiles running
  on). The reel moves one way in 5s, so the frames play forth and back on a 14s cosine ease, cross-faded between
  frames. The sheet loads within half a screen of view; motion plays it until its pause button (the hero's ring, on a
  `--bg` disc, bottom right) stops it; reduced motion draws the first frame still; no JS, no WebGL or a failed load
  keep the poster `img/footer-flower.jpg`, that frame shot from the page at 1920×1080. The earlier closing batik (Remotion's `FooterBatikLoop`,
  `remotion/footer/`, `img/footer-batik*`) is unused.
- **Background batik:** the Gringsing Lidah Api Padma batik as 19,372 dots in one ink (`--text`), behind the hero.
  It is designed in Remotion (`remotion/`, the `BatikHeroEntrance` composition; `remotion/extract_dots.py` takes the
  dots from a photo of the cloth into `remotion/dots.json`) and runs live on the page as a WebGL port of
  `remotion/src/batikDots.ts`, not as a video, so it can answer the pointer. `design-ref/pack_dots.py` packs the dots
  into `img/batik-dots.bin` (8 bytes a dot: position, radius, the pigment's tone, a dark-fill and a highlight flag),
  which the page preloads and fetches. Each frame is three draws of the same points, the cloth and its mirror images
  through the corner Lidah Api, so wide screens show no seam; the cloth is scaled to the hero's height (×1.14) and
  centred on the flame. Remotion's masks keep the nav and the name along the bottom calm.
  - **Entrance:** over 2s (cubic-bezier .32, .08, .45, 1) the dots wake from the flame outwards, bright crests first.
    The clock starts when the dots have loaded, so a slow load still gets it.
  - **Loop:** a seamless 10s cycle of a radial breathing wave from the flame, a diagonal sheen and an inner breath on
    the dark fills, damped over the name. It runs only while the pointer is on the hero. With no pointer on it (it
    left the hero or the window, or hasn't moved since the page loaded), the light eases down to 45% (`DIM`) and the
    loop's clock slows with it to a stop, and then nothing draws until the pointer comes back and both ease up again
    (about a second). The 2s entrance always plays at full light. On load, the one blink of the "vadh" underline (the
    GitHub card's hint, two flashes over 0.9s) waits until 1.5s after the entrance, when the dim has settled (`.hero[data-hint]`).
  - **Pointer:** within 180px the dots swell, brighten and lean away from it, eased in and out (and damped over the
    text like the loop); it fades when the pointer leaves the window. Never on touch.
  - **Strength:** intensity 0.78, Remotion's `BatikHeroWithOverlay` value (made for text on top), at full opacity
    from 1024 up. Below 1024 the name and lead sit mid-hero, out of reach of the masks, so the canvas is at 50%.
    Hero text carries a stacked halo of `--bg` that clears the dots round each glyph.
  - **Cost:** the GPU draws it; the main thread only sets a few uniforms a frame. Pixel ratio 2 at most; nothing
    draws while the tab is hidden, the hero is scrolled away, it is paused or no pointer is on it.
  - **Fallbacks:** no WebGL, a shader that won't link or no data: the canvas hides and the hero sits on the flat `--bg`.
  Placement follows taste-skill (design-taste-frontend): one effect, in the hero only, never a fixed or page-long
  layer behind body copy. It sits at the top of the page, as tall as the hero, full width, scrolls away with it and
  fades out over its last 30%; Work, Experience, About and Contact sit on the flat `--bg`. A pause button (a 30px ring
  in a 44px target, `aria-pressed`, under the right corner bracket) stops it; reduced motion and touch screens
  (`pointer: coarse`) get one still frame (the loop's first, as in the video) and no button.
- **Hiding:** `.js` on `<html>` hides hero parts until the intro starts and removes itself after 3s; if the
  animation clock stalls, everything reverts to visible.

## Accessibility

- `:focus-visible`: 2px `--white` outline, 3px offset. Sections carry no accessible name (a named `<section>` is a
  region landmark); decorative indexes, corners and the canvas are `aria-hidden`. Landmarks: header, nav, main and
  the footer, which also holds the closing flower and its pause button.
- The h1's SplitText letters are `aria-hidden` and the h1 is named "Afridho Tavadhu"; each split gives the "vadh" link's
  line and mask back to the accessibility tree, so no focusable element sits inside `aria-hidden`. The link is named
  "vadh" (`aria-label`) and described by the GitHub card, which is off the Tab path and hidden from the tree while closed.
- Lists styled `list-style: none` outside `<nav>` (cards, project and job rows, contact links) carry `role="list"`, which
  Safari otherwise drops. Link names are unique per target and equal for the same target: hidden context names each
  case study and repo ("Case study (MiniFW-AI)", "GitHub github.com/vadhh/asta"); "All repositories" opens the
  repositories tab and the email address a plain `mailto:`.
- "Skip to content" link to `main#main` is the first focusable element, hidden above the viewport until focused.
- `#top`, `main` and the four sections carry `tabindex="-1"` so anchor jumps move focus (a ring only after a keyboard jump).
- Below 1024px and on any coarse pointer, every link (nav, hero CTAs, `.link`, `.rows .title`, footer; the email below
  1024 only) gets padding + negative margin for a ≥40px hit area without moving the layout.
- `color-scheme: dark` on `:root` and in a `<meta name="color-scheme">`.
- Motion is opt-in: transform transitions, the arrows' nudges, the GitHub card's rise, the "vadh" blink, smooth
  scrolling and the parallax are declared only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; with reduce, colour,
  border, opacity and filter fades (≤200ms) stay, GSAP motion is off and the moving background is drawn once, still.
- CSS: cascade layers `reset, base, components, overrides` (the one-column collapse below 1024 is `overrides`, so it
  needs no `!important`); element resets, not `*`; logical properties except the GitHub card's JS-set `left`/`top` and
  the play triangle. Forced colours: the column rules are redrawn in `CanvasText`.
- **No JS:** the full page renders statically; no background canvas, and the closing flower shows its poster.
- **Link arrows:** literal `↗` in link text sits in `aria-hidden` spans; every `target="_blank"` link carries an
  `.sr-only` " (opens in new tab)". The `.cta::after` arrow uses `content: "↗" / ""` so it isn't announced.
- **Print:** `@media print` switches to black on white, forces every GSAP-hidden card, cover, job row and hero part
  visible, hides the canvas, the closing flower and the skip link, and keeps cards and jobs from breaking across pages. Covers keep a dark
  panel so the light logos stay visible.

## Building the site

`python3 design-ref/build_html.py` writes `index.html` (don't hand-edit it) and asserts card count, hidden
projects, that every listed image, logo, `js/` and `fonts/` file exists, that `img/batik-dots.bin` exists and is
both preloaded and fetched (the preload is what makes `stage.py` ship it), that the closing flower's sheet and
poster exist and are referenced (`stage.py` ships `src`, `href` and `poster` files; its `src=` match also catches the canvas' `data-src`), and that no Google Fonts link is back. Run with plain `python3`, not `-I`.
After re-extracting the dots in `remotion/`, run `python3 design-ref/pack_dots.py` first. To re-trace the flower,
`uv run design-ref/trace_flower.py <reel.mp4>` (the reel stays out of the repo). After changing `FLOWER_FS`, re-shoot
the poster: the page at 1920×1080 under reduced motion, the top mask off, the `.flower-end` element saved as
`img/footer-flower.jpg` (JPEG quality 82).

- `projects.csv`: `order,title,kind,status,year,summary,highlight,stack,repo,monogram,show,image,live`.
  `show` = `featured` → card (max 6; if none, the first six not `ARCHIVED`), `no` → hidden, blank → "More projects"
  (newest year first). `stack` is `;`-separated. `image` → 16:10 cover, 960×600 in `img/projects/` (current covers are lossless WebP; they have transparency).
- `experience.csv`: `order,type,role,company,start,end,location,summary,highlight,stack,link,logo`.
  Dates `YYYY-MM` or `Present`. `logo` → 96×96 PNG(s) in `img/logos/`, `;`-separated for side-by-side tiles.
- Copy (hero lead, section leads, about, whoami, facts, offer, email, links) lives in `content.py`. A `\n` is a
  desktop-only line break.
- Deploys to Vercel on push: `vercel.json` runs the build, then `design-ref/stage.py` publishes only the pages and the
  files they reference. Never `design-ref/`: it holds crops of a third-party screenshot.
