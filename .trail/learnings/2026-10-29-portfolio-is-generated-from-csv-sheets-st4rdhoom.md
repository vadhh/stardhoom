## Decided
- index.html is generated by `python3 design-ref/build_html.py`. Don't hand-edit it. Content lives in content/experience.csv, content/projects.csv and the copy constants in design-ref/content.py.
- The same content builds the FigCraft desktop and mobile frames (build_portfolio.py and build_mobile.py, through fc.py). content.py does no I/O, so the HTML generator runs without FigCraft.
- projects.csv `show`: `featured` = card (max 6), `no` = hidden, blank = "More projects" list. Freelance rows in experience.csv are left out of Experience on purpose; they appear under Work.
- Vertical grid rules are three 1px linear-gradients on `.wrap::before`. Text blocks that span columns 3-4 (`.c34`) get the bg colour to hide the rule.

## Tried and ruled out
- Fetching the CSVs at runtime: breaks on file://, hides content from crawlers, flashes empty on load.
- A repeating-linear-gradient for the rules: the first rule lands on a half pixel and disappears.

## Watch out
- Run the design-ref scripts with plain `python3`, not `python3 -I`. Isolated mode drops the script dir from sys.path, so `from fc import *` fails.
- Hidden `<br class="wide">` needs a space before it, or words merge on mobile.
- If the owner edits a CSV in Numbers and exports again, it can overwrite script-applied edits. Check the file's mtime before assuming your edit is still there.
- Upload only index.html (plus resume.pdf and snake.html). design-ref/ contains crops of a third-party reference screenshot.
